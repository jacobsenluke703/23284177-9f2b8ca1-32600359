02

Text

Save a dated message or publish it as a monochrome card.

The online version is rendered into the same 128 × 64 format as an image. A locally saved text is used only when no image is assigned to that date.

03

Audio

Convert audio to compact mono files for the device.

Audio is converted to 16 kHz, mono, 8-bit PCM and trimmed to 20 seconds. Up to 12 sound files can be stored on the device.

Destination settings
